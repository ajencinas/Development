## Intent

A promotional launch video for "GridVoice" — a voice AI agent built for
utilities (electric, gas, water). Concept brief, no website to capture:
the product name is an invented neutral placeholder since the user named
no brand. The story angle is "The 2:14 AM Call": a storm-night outage
told through the agent handling it — a customer calls in the dark, the
agent answers instantly, reports the outage, dispatches context, and
follows up — then the video pulls back to show the same thing happening
across thousands of simultaneous calls. Tone: calm, reassuring,
grounded in the utility world (storm, grid, night shift) — explicitly
NOT the generic purple-gradient "AI-powered support" SaaS promo.

## Customizations

- Scale beat: count-up on simultaneous calls handled (thousands at once)
  to prove capacity during storm events.
- Voice-waveform motif as the visual identity of the agent — the calm,
  steady element on a storm-dark canvas.
- Night-shift timestamp motif (2:14 AM …) structuring the story beats.

## Notes

- Sell, not show: promotional intent stated by the user ("promotion
  video"). No-capture path — there is no site; the design system comes
  from a shipped preset.
- Avoid: robot avatars, chat bubbles, stock call-center footage,
  buzzword walls.
- Autonomous run: user asked once and is not present; decisions are
  stated with receipts in the heads-up summary.
