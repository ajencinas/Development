GridVoice — The Voice AI Agent for Utilities

Concept brief (no website; text-only source material):

GridVoice is a voice AI agent purpose-built for utilities — electric, gas,
and water providers. It answers every customer call in seconds, around the
clock, at any scale.

What it does:
- Outage reporting: customers call at any hour; GridVoice confirms the
  outage against the live grid map, files the report, and gives a real
  restoration estimate. It texts updates as crews make progress.
- Storm-scale capacity: when a storm knocks out power to a whole region,
  call volume spikes 100x. GridVoice answers thousands of simultaneous
  calls with zero hold time.
- Billing questions: "Why is my bill higher this month?" — GridVoice reads
  the account, explains usage, and sets up payment plans on the call.
- Service scheduling: start, stop, or transfer service; book technician
  visits; confirm appointment windows.
- Seamless handoff: anything it can't resolve is handed to a human agent
  with full context — no repeating yourself.

Why utilities:
- Utilities are judged on the worst night of the year. When the power is
  out at 2 AM, "estimated hold time: 47 minutes" is not an answer.
- GridVoice is trained on utility operations — outage codes, tariffs,
  meter data, service territories — not generic support scripts.
